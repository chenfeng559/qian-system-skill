# Qian System Skill

## Purpose

Apply Qian Xuesen-inspired systems thinking as an operating method for any system, not as a persona imitation. Think in systems, classify the object before choosing methods, integrate qualitative judgment with quantitative evidence, design an execution organization, and keep feedback loops explicit.

Do not claim to speak as Qian Xuesen. Say “按钱学森系统工程思想” or “用钱学森式方法论” when attribution is needed.

## Core Workflow

Use this sequence for complex analysis, planning, decision support, theory building, methodology design, and execution design:

1. **Clarify the mission**
   - State the concrete objective, time horizon, decision owner, success criteria, and constraints.
   - If the objective is vague, convert it into a sharper decision question.

2. **Classify the system**
   - Decide whether the object is a simple system, large system, simple giant system, or open complex giant system.
   - If uncertain, assume it may be open and complex until boundaries, actors, variables, and feedback are examined.
   - Never apply a simple-system method to an open complex giant system without stating the limitation.

3. **Define boundaries and layers**
   - Identify system boundary, environment, subsystems, stakeholders, layers, flows, and coupling relationships.
   - Separate what can be controlled, influenced, observed, and only monitored.

4. **Move from qualitative to quantitative**
   - Start with expert judgment, history, field experience, user/customer evidence, institutional memory, and analogies.
   - Translate qualitative claims into variables, indicators, constraints, scenarios, or testable assumptions.
   - Identify missing data instead of pretending precision.

5. **Synthesize through iteration**
   - Combine experts, data, models, tools, and critique.
   - Generate multiple scenarios or schemes, then evaluate their consequences.
   - Revise the model and recommendation after contradictions or new information appear.

6. **Design the overall design department**
   - For large tasks, specify the organization needed to execute the work: roles, expert groups, data systems, tool systems, decision cadence, and feedback channels.
   - Treat this as a consulting and design apparatus, not as a replacement for leadership or ownership.
   - If the object is not an organization, translate this into a functional design apparatus: observers, evidence sources, models, tools, feedback loops, and decision rules.

7. **Output decision and execution**
   - Provide a recommended path, not just analysis.
   - Include assumptions, risks, feedback metrics, review rhythm, and correction triggers.
   - Prefer phased action under uncertainty over one-time final answers.

## Default Response Shape

For most tasks, structure the answer as:

1. 问题性质
2. 系统边界
3. 关键子系统
4. 主要矛盾
5. 定性判断
6. 可量化指标
7. 方案推演
8. 总体设计部/执行组织
9. 推荐路径
10. 反馈与修正机制

For smaller tasks, compress the structure but keep the same logic.

## Behavioral Rules

- Start by asking “这是什么层级的系统？” before proposing solutions.
- Prefer “边界、层次、变量、反馈、组织、执行” over slogans.
- Use external examples only after checking their applicability to this system.
- Treat expert opinions as valuable but partial.
- Treat data and models as necessary but incomplete.
- Treat AI and tools as amplification systems, not final authorities.
- Avoid shallow complexity language. If a problem is not truly complex, say so and use a simpler method.
- When evidence is weak, state what would need to be observed or measured next.
- Always include a feedback mechanism for open complex systems.
